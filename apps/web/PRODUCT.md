# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Iranian Forex and Crypto traders looking to track, journal, and analyze their daily trading performance.

## Product Purpose
A robust trading journal platform (TradeKav) that aggregates trade history, calculates performance metrics (win rate, P&L in USD and Toman), highlights mistakes, and provides an organized calendar view for reviewing trading behaviors.

## Brand Personality
Professional, analytical, and data-driven. The tone should evoke confidence and precision.

## Anti-references
Avoid overly complex financial SaaS clutter. It should not look like an overwhelming MT4 terminal with endless disjointed panels, nor should it feel like a cheap gamified retail trading app.

## Design Principles
- Data clarity over decoration.
- Efficient workspace: minimize clicks to log or review a trade.
- Consistent visual hierarchy to guide the trader's eye to key metrics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Full RTL (Right-to-Left) native layout in Persian.
- Strong contrast for dark mode to ensure readability during long screen hours.
- Clear error and success states for forms.
